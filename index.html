Intensive Care Units of Canada

A community-maintained map of critical care units, by the Canadian Critical Care Society (CCCS) — for intensivists, ICU nurses, RRTs and every critical care colleague.

Northern note — Yukon, Northwest Territories & Nunavut have no tertiary ICU; critical care is delivered there via medevac and transfer to southern referral centres.
